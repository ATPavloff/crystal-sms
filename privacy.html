Privacy Policy: Crystal Scheduling Alerts

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Crystal Scheduling Alerts is operated by APX Strategies ("we," "us"). It is a personal text message service for the account owner only.

What we collect

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your calendar details, used only to write reminders and alerts for you
  • Basic message delivery records, such as sent time and delivery status

How we use it

We use this information only to send you reminders and schedule alerts that you asked for, and to keep the service working. We do not use it for marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your phone number and opt-in consent are not shared with anyone else. Our messaging provider, Twilio, delivers the texts on our behalf and handles them only for that purpose.

Security and retention

We keep your information only as long as the service needs it, and we protect it with reasonable safeguards.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to end text messages. To ask us to delete your information, email atpavloff@gmail.com.
